iPhone · TestFlight

Apple's own app for testing. Free, no code needed, and new versions arrive by themselves.

1 · Get TestFlight from the App Store
2 · Join the In the Binder beta
  1. Tap 1 on your iPhone and install TestFlight. Skip this if you already have it.
  2. Come back and tap 2, then Accept and Install in TestFlight.
  3. Open In the Binder from your home screen. New test versions show up in TestFlight; turn on Automatic Updates there.

Stuck? Open link 2 on the iPhone itself, not on a computer. If TestFlight says the beta is full or not accepting testers, email support@inthebinder.com and we'll let you in.
