Press kit

In the Binder is a free app that turns a trading card collection into real binders on your phone. Point the camera at a card and it's identified, filed into its set's binder and valued, page by page, in every language.

What it does

  • Scan by picture: recognition runs on the phone and reads the artwork, so sleeves and binder pages are fine.
  • Every set as a real binder with nine pockets a page; empty pockets show what's missing.
  • One binder per language, so Japanese and French prints are never mixed with English ones.
  • Market value, eBay buy and sell links, price alerts, graded slabs and grading picks.
  • Quick fill, face-to-face trading with QR codes, and mix-and-match themes.

En français

In the Binder est une application gratuite qui transforme une collection de cartes à collectionner en vrais classeurs sur téléphone. On vise une carte, elle est reconnue, rangée dans le classeur de sa série et estimée, page par page, dans toutes les langues. Sans abonnement, compte facultatif.
