Things other card apps don't do

Built by a collector who wanted more than a list.

GRADING

AI centring check before you submit

Hold a card under the camera and the borders are measured automatically. You see the best grade centring alone allows at PSA, BGS and CGC, before you pay for grading. A manual mode lets you nudge every edge by 0.1 mm.

SLABS

Tap a slab, done

Hold an NFC-chipped slab to the back of your phone and the certificate is read instantly, no buttons. No chip? Point the camera at the label and the cert number is read for you.

6 GAMES

Six card games, never mixed

Pokémon, One Piece, Lorcana, Dragon Ball Super Fusion World, Magic and Yu-Gi-Oh! each get their own binders, slabs and sealed product. Every printing has its own pocket: normal, parallel, foil, alternate art. A game downloads only when you open it, so the app stays small.

8 LANGUAGES

Japanese, Korean and Chinese Pokémon sets

Separate Pokémon binders for English, French, Italian, Spanish, German, Japanese, Korean and Chinese printings, with thousands of Asian cards recognised by the scanner.

Scan by picture

Recognition runs on your phone. It reads the artwork, not just the text, so glare, sleeves and whole 9-pocket pages are fine. If it isn't sure, it asks rather than guessing. It reads printed codes like OP01-003 or LOB-EN001 too, and learns the look of your own cards from every scan you confirm.

Binders, set by set

Each set is a leather binder with nine cards a page. Empty pockets show what you're still hunting. Normal, reverse holo, holo, parallels and foils are tracked separately.

Questions

What is In the Binder?

In the Binder is a free Android app for trading card collectors. You scan cards with your phone camera, file them into binders set by set, check centring before sending cards for grading, track graded slabs and see what your collection is worth.

Which card games does it support?

Pokémon (including Japanese, Korean and Chinese prints), plus the English editions of One Piece, Disney Lorcana, Dragon Ball Super Fusion World, Magic: The Gathering and Yu-Gi-Oh!. Each game has its own binders, slabs and sealed product, never mixed. A game's card list downloads the first time you open it.

Is In the Binder free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use.

Does it work with Japanese, Korean and Chinese cards?

Yes. The scanner and binders support English, French, Italian, Spanish, German, Japanese, Korean and Chinese prints.

Can it help me before sending cards to PSA?

The centring check measures the borders of your card from a photo and compares them with the centring limits used by PSA, BGS and CGC. It is a guide only; the final grade is always the grading company's decision.

Is it available on iPhone?

Yes, as a beta. Anyone can join the iPhone beta through Apple's TestFlight app from inthebinder.com/beta, and the App Store release is coming soon. The web version also works on any iPhone.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Your collection is stored on your phone and the app works offline. Prices update when you are online.
