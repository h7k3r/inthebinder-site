Things other card apps don't do

Built by a collector who wanted more than a list.

GRADING

AI centring check before you submit

Hold a card under the camera and the borders are measured automatically. You see the best grade centring alone allows at PSA, BGS and CGC, before you pay for grading. A manual mode lets you nudge every edge by 0.1 mm.

SLABS

Tap a slab, done

Hold an NFC-chipped slab to the back of your phone and the certificate is read instantly, no buttons. No chip? Point the camera at the label and the cert number is read for you.

8 LANGUAGES

Japanese, Korean and Chinese sets included

Separate binders for English, French, Italian, Spanish, German, Japanese, Korean and Chinese printings, with thousands of Asian cards recognised by the scanner.

SWITCHING

Bring your collection with you

Import your whole PriceCharting collection in one go, with nothing lost: anything that can't be matched is kept with its value. Back up to a single file anytime.

Built by a collector, for collectors

Everything you'd do with a real binder, a price guide and a notebook, in one place, and it works offline.

◎

Scan by picture

Recognition runs on your phone. It reads the artwork, not just the text, so glare, sleeves and whole 9-pocket pages are fine. If it isn't sure, it asks rather than guessing.

▦

Binders, set by set

Each set is a leather binder with nine cards a page. Empty pockets show what you're still hunting. Normal, reverse holo and holo are tracked separately.

↗

Know what it's worth

Market prices for each printing, price history over time, and your whole portfolio's value at a glance.

▣

Graded slabs

Track PSA, BGS, CGC, ACE, AURA and more. Read the certificate from the label or tap an NFC-chipped slab.

⌕

Search like it's printed

Type "070/084" or a name with a typo, and it still finds the card.

❖

Sealed product too

Booster boxes, ETBs and tins by barcode, alongside your cards and slabs.

Secure by design

A collection can be worth thousands. In the Binder never asks you to hand it to a server: there's no account to hack and no cloud database to leak.

  • No sign-up, no login, no password to leak
  • Your collection is stored only on your phone and works fully offline
  • Card recognition runs on the device; your photos are never uploaded
  • Only the permissions it needs: camera to scan, NFC to read slabs
  • Distributed through Google Play, signed and checked by Google
  • Everything online is encrypted (HTTPS only, HSTS)
  • Your data, your file: export or back up whenever you like
